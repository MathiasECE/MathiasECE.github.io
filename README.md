## 🛠️ Featured Engineering Projects

### 1. 🏎️ IMOCA Cockpit VR Experience — 4CAD Group
- **Context:** Industrial Engineering Internship · Vendée Globe 2028 campaign (Skipper Benjamin Dutreux).
- **Highlights:** Real-time 3D Digital Twin for cockpit ergonomic validation; motion sensor-driven VR walkthrough presented at **Global Industrie 2026**.
- **Keywords:** `Virtual Reality`, `Motion Sensing`, `3D Rendering Engine`, `KeyShot`.

### 2. 🤖 [Drawbot — Autonomous Plotter Robot](https://github.com/MathiasECE/drawbot-esp32)
- **Context:** Academic Engineering Project · ING2 S2.
- **Highlights:** Differential-drive kinematic robot, dual PID closed-loop control (encoder interrupts), pen-offset mathematical compensation, and embedded asynchronous Wi-Fi web interface.
- **Keywords:** `C++`, `ESP32`, `PID Control`, `Kinematics`, `FreeRTOS`.

### 3. 🕹️ [Fruit Ninja on FPGA](https://github.com/MathiasECE/fruit-ninja-fpga)
- **Context:** Academic Engineering Project · ING2 S1.
- **Highlights:** Complete arcade game implemented in **pure RTL VHDL** (zero software/CPU overhead) at 25 MHz on Intel MAX 10 FPGA. Custom 640×480 @ 60 Hz VGA pipeline, ultrasonic contactless hand tracker, and fixed-point trigonometric collision math.
- **Keywords:** `VHDL`, `Intel MAX 10`, `RTL Design`, `Quartus Prime`, `VGA 60Hz`.
